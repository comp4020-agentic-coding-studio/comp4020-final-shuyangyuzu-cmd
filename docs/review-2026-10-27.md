# Stamina/menu and voice review — Codex, 2026-10-27

I asked Codex to review Claude's round and a real voice failure with a friend. I confirmed both devices were on the same Wi-Fi. The review started at clean HEAD 9cc3f15, green CI 40499878566 and production build f538515b9e. Fly's secret-name list was empty. Missing TURN limits connectivity, but does not establish the cause of this same-Wi-Fi failure.

## Reproduced defects and repairs

- Playback rejection was invisible: remote audio relied on autoplay alone. The client now calls play, handles failure with Enable sound, and wraps streamless tracks in a MediaStream.
- An authenticated private offer arriving before the public roster was discarded. Per-peer signal queues now refresh/revalidate membership and serialize negotiation while retaining room/call generation checks.
- A lost initial offer was never resent. Joining and newly added receiving peers now announce readiness; the deterministic initiator resends. Real-media verification exposed two gaps in the first fix: readiness racing a pending send, and the original caller acquiring a peer after joining alone. Additional regressions protect both.
- SSE unsubscribe ran both on abort and after the loop. An old second cleanup could delete a replacement stream's map entry. Three regressions failed before fixing voice, room and private-inbox cleanup to run once and remove only its own set; a fourth protects another still-live tab.
- A global order-idempotency lookup did not check owner or original item/variant. Two regressions reproduced returning another player's order and accepting a conflicting variant. Both now return 409 rather than disclose or misrepresent an earlier order.

Joined membership is still different from media connectivity. The UI separately shows connected peer counts, incoming audio packet activity, microphone activity when available, playback blocking and Reconnect audio. Raw audio, SDP, ICE addresses and credentials are not added to logs. No TURN service is purchased or configured.
