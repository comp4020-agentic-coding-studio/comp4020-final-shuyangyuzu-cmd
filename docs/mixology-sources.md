# Mixology book: sources and planning choices

Added by Codex on 2026-10-23 after I chose a counter-side cocktail assistant as a practical use for the tavern. Recipe proportions were checked against the primary pages below. Preparation instructions are rewritten, and the glass illustrations are original CSS. No remote recipe service, imported artwork, new dependency or account is needed.

## Alcohol-free choices

Source: [University of Nebraska–Lincoln, Mocktail Mixology](https://crec.unl.edu/alcohol-and-other-drug-education/mocktail-mixology-drinks-and-recipes/).

- **Husker Mule:** the source offers ginger beer/ale, lime and soda without fixed measures. The book uses ≈120 ml alcohol-free ginger beer, ≈15 ml lime and ≈60 ml soda per glass, explicitly identified as house planning measures. Mint is optional.
- **Sweetheart Serenade:** adapted from Husker Sweetheart Serenade. Lime 7.5 ml, syrup 15 ml, cranberry juice 80 ml and coconut water 90 ml per glass; metric measures are rounded, and cranberry juice is the selected option.
- **Cornhusker Crush:** the source serves two. The book retains that base yield: coconut water 120 ml, sliced strawberries 1 cup, mint 12 leaves, lime 30 ml, honey 15 ml and soda 240 ml. Scaling divides by the two-glass base before multiplying. For planning, cup = 240 ml and tablespoon = 15 ml. Strawberries remain cups; mint leaves are not converted to sprigs.

## Scope of the helper

Pantry ticks mean an ingredient is available, not that there is enough for the entire plan. Up to 20 glasses per recipe are supported. Required ingredients are summed only when both ingredient and unit match; teaspoons and bar spoons remain separate. Ice is assumed available, optional garnish is listed separately, and neither is included in matching or the shopping totals. Equipment lists describe the method rather than purchasing quantities.

Pantry, party quantities and bought checkboxes are saved under a dedicated browser-local key. They never change a game balance, create an order or send a pantry to the server. Clearing browser storage removes them; other devices do not inherit them. This feature does not complete the separately paused food-ordering system. Clipboard failure shows selectable text rather than reporting a false copy success.
