A little place to be together. Meet friends, play at a table, or share something worth cooking.

1. Get started

Choose Sign up, Log in, or Continue as guest. Your account username must be unique; your character nickname does not. Choose your clothing colour and skin tone under Your character.

Save the recovery codes displayed after registration somewhere private. To reset a forgotten password, enter your username and an unused recovery code. There is no email recovery. If you are still logged in, use the account controls to generate replacement codes; the old batch then stops working.

Accounts keep your character across devices. Logging in elsewhere signs out the earlier session. Guests belong to the current browser; clearing its storage can lose access. At signup, choose whether to keep your current guest character.

2. Find your seat

Walk with WASD, arrow keys, the on-screen arrows, or by clicking the floor. Click a chair to approach it and sit. Use Stand up to leave an ordinary dining seat. Other people occupy space too: choose a free path.

The top bar shows who is here and your stamina. Use the speech bubble to speak publicly to the tavern. Messages are not private.

3. Choose a game

Online tables are shared with other players. Offline means playing against computer opponents, not playing without internet. All chips and winnings are virtual.

TableJoining and play
Las VegasThe first seated player hosts and approves later arrivals. Everyone readies up, then the host starts. Roll, choose a face, and place all dice of that face at its casino. Equal dice counts cancel when paying out. Play four rounds; the host starts each next round. Your cumulative winnings stay private until the final result. The first starter is random.
Texas Hold’emSit at a free seat; no invitation needed. Make the offered fold/check/call/bet actions on your turn. New players wait until the current hand ends, even if a chair is empty. A waiting notification is an invitation to try again, not a guaranteed reservation.
BlackjackSit directly and place your wager. Hit takes another card; Stand ends your turn. Use Double when offered. Aim to beat the dealer without exceeding 21. Players and NPCs act in sequence. New arrivals wait for the round to finish.
All Fives dominoesSit opposite your opponent. Each four-seat fixture has two independent pairs. Start a new match manually when ready. Match a tile to a legal highlighted open end; the spinner can introduce branches under the table’s rules. Score when the exposed-end total is a multiple of five. Draw when required. A replacement opponent starts a fresh match, not the departed player’s hand.

Open each table’s Rules for its detailed rules and available actions. To join a friend, enter the tavern and choose the same online table.

4. Leaving is different from stepping away

Leave table / Exit game gives up your membership and returns your character to a walkable place in the tavern. An ongoing game may fold or hand the departing turn to a computer so others can continue. Joining restrictions still apply until a card-game round ends.

Back to the bar temporarily leaves the chair while reserving your game seat, marked with your name and Away. Someone else cannot take that reserved seat. Return through your own seat. In Vegas, use the offered away choice if you want a computer to play your turns; resuming gives control back to you.

Temporary reservations are not permanent storage: observe the room’s grace-period message. Closing a tab or losing connection may eventually release a seat. Saving an account does not reserve a chair forever.

5. A break at the counter

Click the book on the counter. Choose Meals or Drinks, view a dish, choose any variants, and confirm the order. You can hold one meal and one drink at a time.

Sit at a dining chair or bar stool, then click the overhead Eat, Drink, or Eat & Drink prompt. A short animation locks other actions while you finish. Game chairs do not qualify as dining seats.

Meals restore 70 percentage points of stamina; drinks restore 30, capped at 100%. Stamina falls while you are online and does not change the game rules. Recipe calorie estimates are informational, separate from this fictional mechanic. Full stamina does not prevent eating.

6. The Recipe Book

Discover finds house and community recipes. Filter Food/Drinks and search names, ingredients, tags or authors. View a recipe to read its ingredients and steps. Change servings to scale numeric measures; “to taste” remains unscaled. On desktop the two pages scroll separately. Zoom or drag a photo to inspect it without editing the author’s recipe.

My pantry records available ingredients. Save recipe bookmarks a recipe. Add to party plan keeps a snapshot for your plan and shopping quantities; later author edits do not silently change that snapshot. Different units are not added together.

Log in to publish under My Recipes. Choose Food or Drink, add ingredients, quantities and steps, optionally upload a photo, and preview before publishing. Adjust the photo’s position and zoom in the editor, then save. Draft changes remain private until published. Edit or delete only your own recipes; batch deletion is available there. Use only photos you have permission to share. Report inappropriate recipes using their Report button.

Community recipes are real-world reading and planning tools. Publishing one does not add it to the tavern’s orderable Meals or Drinks.

7. If something goes wrong

Wait for an action to finish before retrying. If a seat becomes unavailable, choose another or wait for the next round. An old tab may need a fresh login after account takeover. Preserve recovery codes and never post them in chat or recipes.

Voice calling has been discontinued because reliable real-device audio was not achieved. No microphone is needed to use the tavern.

When reporting a problem, include the game, action, approximate time and the build shown on screen. Do not include passwords, recovery codes or session secrets.
